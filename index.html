Last updated: September 15, 2026

Snakes Ladders does not collect, store, or share any personal information or device data.

Because we do not collect data or use analytics, tracking cookies, or external ad networks, no third-party services are integrated to gather your personal information.

Because Snakes Ladders collects no data whatsoever, it is completely safe for users of all ages, including children under the age of 13.
